# Privacy Policy — Rei Cinza

**Last updated:** October 4, 2026
**App:** Rei Cinza (package `com.reicinza.diario`)
**Controller:** Renato Carvalho Queiroz — contact: sobreviventeuniversal@gmail.com

## In one sentence

Rei Cinza does not collect, transmit or share any personal data **of yours**. Everything you
write stays on your device. The app's only communication with the outside world is with
**Google Play**, and only to process an optional purchase — described below.

## What data the app touches

- **What you write.** Your daily records, notes, scores and your name are stored only in the
  app's internal database, on your device. The app sends none of it anywhere — it has no server
  of its own, and there is no account and no sync.
- **There is no account.** No sign-up, login, e-mail or password in the app. The app never asks
  who you are. (A purchase made on Google Play is tied to your Google account, like any store
  purchase — that is handled by Google itself, not by the app.)
- **There is no usage collection.** The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking
  libraries. We do not record which screens you open, for how long, or how often.
- **Notifications.** If you enable reminders, the operating system schedules local notices on the
  device itself. No content is sent to anyone for that.

## In-app purchases

The app offers **one optional, single purchase** (unlocking the nine study texts). About it:

- **Google Play processes the payment**, through the official Android billing library. The app
  **never sees, receives or stores** card data, bank account details or billing addresses —
  none of that passes through it.
- **What the app checks** is only Google Play's answer about that purchase: whether it exists,
  whether it is active, and whether it can be unlocked or restored on this device.
- **None of your journal data is sent along with it.** The purchase check carries nothing you
  have written, and does not identify you to us.
- **You can restore the purchase** at any time inside the app. Your Google account holds the
  record of the purchase — which is why it can be recovered on another device, and why we cannot
  delete it on our side: it is not on our side.
- **Declining or cancelling the purchase affects nothing else.** The daily record, the compass,
  the foundations, the history, the statistics, the export and the backup all keep working in
  full.

## Permissions and why they exist

| Permission | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Notifications | to show the morning reminder (7 am) and the closing one (9:30 pm), if you enable them |
| Vibration | to vibrate with the reminder |
| Restart with the device | to reschedule your reminders after the phone is restarted |
| Storage (Android 12 and below) | to save the backup file or the exported notes, in the folder you choose |
| Internet | to talk to **Google Play** to process, verify and restore the optional purchase. It is also used by the system's notification library, which ships messaging components. The app **does not send what you write**: there is no server, account or journal sync. |
| In-app billing | to allow the optional purchase through Google Play, and nothing else |

You can revoke any permission in Android settings. Without the notification permission, the app
still works — it simply does not remind you. Without the billing permission, everything still
works too — you simply cannot buy.

## Export and backup: where your data goes

Export and backup are **your actions**: the app generates the files and the system asks which
folder to save them to (including cloud services you may have installed, such as Google Drive).
From that point on, the file is yours to keep, under the rules of whichever service you chose.
The app has no access to those files once they are written.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects data from no one, of any age.

## Deleting your data

All journal data lives on the device. Uninstalling the app erases everything. Since we hold no
copy, there is nothing to ask us to delete — there is no "our side".

The record of a purchase, however, is held by **Google Play**, tied to your Google account, and
follows its rules. We cannot delete it, and we hold no copy of it here.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts doing anything different from what is described here, this policy will be
updated and the date above will change. Data collection does not change without you being told on
the store listing itself.
