## One-sentence summary

Rei Cinza does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data **of yours**. Nothing leaves your phone.
The only communication the app has with the outside is with **Google Play**, and only to process an
optional purchase — described further below.

## What data the app stores — and where

- **What you write.** Your daily Records, the Commitments you mark, the scores, the history and the
  statistics are stored only in the app's internal database, on your device. None of it is sent to
  any server — the app has no server of its own, and there is no account and no synchronization.
- **Your name, if you provide it.** During the first setup the app offers an **optional** field for
  your name, used only to address you by name on the screens. It stays in the same local database
  and is deleted along with everything else.
- **There is no account.** There is no sign-up, login, e-mail, or password. The app does not ask for
  identification. (A purchase made on Google Play is tied to your Google account, like any purchase
  in the store — that is handled by Google itself, not by the app.)
- **There is no usage collection.** The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking
  libraries — verified in the app's dependencies on October 14, 2026. We do not record which screens
  you open, for how long, or how often.
- **Notifications.** The reminders (morning, at 7 a.m., and end of day, at 9:30 p.m.) are **local**
  alerts, scheduled by the system itself on your device. No content is sent to third parties for
  this, and you can turn them off.

## What the app does NOT do

- it does **not** collect personal, usage, or diagnostic data;
- it does **not** use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking services;
- it does **not** request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or an advertising identifier;
- it does **not** send server notifications (there is no messaging service of ours);
- it does **not** share any data with third parties.

## In-app purchases

The app offers **one single, optional purchase** — the **Doctrine of the nine chambers**, which is
the study content of each one. About it:

- **The payment is processed by Google Play**, through Android's official billing library. The app
  **does not see, receive, or store** card, bank account, or billing address data — none of that
  passes through it.
- **What the app queries** is only Google Play's answer about that purchase: whether it exists,
  whether it is active, and whether it can be unlocked or restored on this device.
- **No data of yours from the diary is sent along.** The purchase query carries neither what you
  write nor any identification of you to us.
- **You can restore the purchase** at any time inside the app. Your Google account controls the
  purchase record — which is why it is recoverable on another device, and why we cannot delete it on
  our side: it is not on our side.
- **Declining or cancelling the purchase does not affect the rest.** Daily Record, Streak, Compass,
  the chambers and their states (cold, smouldering, lit, tempered), history, statistics, export, and
  backup keep working in full. **All of it is free**, with no ads and no subscription.

## Permissions and why they exist

| Permission | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Notifications | show the morning (7 a.m.) and end-of-day (9:30 p.m.) reminders, if you enable them |
| Vibration | vibrate along with the reminder |
| Restart on device boot | reschedule your reminders after the phone is restarted |
| Storage (Android 12 or earlier) | save the backup file or the export, in the location you choose |
| Internet | talk to **Google Play** to process, verify, and restore the optional purchase |
| In-app billing | allow the optional purchase through Google Play, and only that |

The app **does not use the internet for anything other than the purchase**: there is no server,
account, or diary synchronization. Besides these, the package declares permissions that the **system
components themselves** declare (notifications and a badge counter on some phone manufacturers);
none of them sends your data anywhere.

You can revoke any permission in the Android settings. Without the notification permission the app
keeps working — it simply does not warn you. Without the billing permission, everything keeps
working as well — it is simply not possible to buy.

## Export and backup: where your data goes

Export and backup are **actions you take**: the app generates the files and the system asks which
folder to save them in (including cloud services you may have installed, such as Google Drive). From
that point on, safekeeping of the file is yours and follows the rules of the service you choose. The
app has no access to those files after saving them.

## Deleting your data

- **Delete everything through the app** (Settings): removes your Records, the chambers, the
  statistics, and the name you provided. The **settings** (language and purchase entitlement) are
  preserved, because they are not your content.
- **Uninstalling the app:** deletes all local data.
- Since we have no copy at all, there is nothing to ask us to delete on our side — there is no "our
  side".
- A **backup file** you kept outside the device stays where you put it: deleting the data in the app
  does not delete that file.
- The **purchase record** stays with Google Play, tied to your Google account, and follows its
  rules. We cannot delete it, and we have no copy of it here.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone, of any age.

If the app comes to do anything different from what is described here, this policy will be updated
